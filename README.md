Al-Asturlab Al-Andalusi is an independent, museum-grade astronomical software suite recreating the 11th-century Toledo & Cordoba planispheric astrolabes (inspired by master astronomers *Ibrahim ibn Said al-Sahli* and *Al-Zarqali*). Built for cultural institutions, museums, planetariums, horology houses, and luxury digital heritage collectors.

---

## 🏛️ The Four Master Pillars

### 1. ⚜️ The Astrolabe Hands-On Simulator (محاكي الأسطرلاب الحي)
- **Flippable Dual-Face Architecture:** Seamless 3D flip between Front Face (*Mater, Limb, Tympan, Rete, Rule*) and Back Face (*Alidade with Pinholes, Shadow Square, Sine Quadrant, Calendar Scale*).
- **Physical Drag Dynamics:** Tactile inertia drag on Rete star daggers and Alidade sighting bar with acoustic ratchet and brass detent clicks.
- **Real-Time Spherical Astronomy:**
  - Solar Altitude and Azimuth tracking.
  - Zodiac Ecliptic Longitude.
  - Exact Spherical Trigonometry Qibla Direction (سمت القبلة).
  - Umbra Recta (الظل المبسوط) and Umbra Versa (الظل المنكوس) for terrestrial and altitude surveying.
  - Astronomical Islamic Prayer Times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) computed natively.
- **Interchangeable Regional Plates (الصفائح):** Pre-calibrated stereographic tympans for Cordoba (37.8°), Toledo (39.8°), Seville (37.3°), Mecca (21.4°), Medina (24.4°), Cairo (30.0°), Damascus (33.5°), Baghdad (33.3°), Paris (48.8°), and London (51.5°).

### 2. 🌌 The Sky Observatory & Star Navigation (القبة السماوية والاستدلال بالنجوم)
- **Interactive Night Sky Dome:** Real 3D celestial sphere synchronized with the astrolabe.
- **60+ Major Navigational Stars:** Featuring authentic Arabic etymology (*الجدي، الشعرى اليمانية، سهيل اليماني، النسر الواقع، الدبران، السماك الرامح، قلب العقرب، العيوق*).
- **Constellation Linkages:** Real line mappings for *Ursa Major (بنات نعش الكبرى)*, *Orion (الجوزاء)*, and *The Summer Triangle (مثلث الصيف)*.
- **Stereographic Projection Overlay:** Visual explanation of how 3D spherical coordinates are mapped onto a 2D brass plate without angular distortion.

### 3. 📜 The Celestial Navigation Academy (أكاديمية الاستدلال الفلكي)
- **Lesson I:** How the Astrolabe Captures Space (Stereographic projection geometry).
- **Lesson II:** Finding Latitude via Polaris Sight (نجم الجدي وخط العرض).
- **Lesson III:** Extracting Exact Night Time from Stellar Altitudes (النسر الواقع وساعات الليل).
- **Lesson IV:** Qibla Direction & Surveying Mountains with the Shadow Square.

### 4. 📐 Engineering CAD Blueprints & Museum Authentication (المخططات والشهادة)
- High-precision horological CAD specification tables (diameter, alloy composition, plate tolerance, obliquity of ecliptic $\varepsilon = 23^\circ 26' 21.4''$).
- One-click JSON export of complete astrolabe engineering blueprints.
- Printable illuminated Certificate of Authenticity with custom imperial red wax seal.
